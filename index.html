How we work

From first survey to long-term support

Every property is different, so every job starts on site. We look at how the building is used, agree what the system needs to do, and then design around that.

More about our approach

  1. Survey

    We walk the site with you and note the risks, the routes and the equipment already in place.

  2. Design

    You receive a clear proposal: device layout, equipment list, cabling plan and price.

  3. Install

    Neat, labelled installation, coordinated with other trades when the building is still under construction.

  4. Handover and support

    We test everything, train your team and stay available for maintenance and upgrades.
