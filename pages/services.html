Services

Systems that control, watch over and secure your building

We handle design, supply, installation, integration and maintenance. Take one service on its own or have all three delivered as a single coordinated system.

01

Building automation

A building automation system brings lighting, cooling, power and plant equipment under one set of controls. It runs to a schedule, responds to occupancy, and tells you when something needs attention, which cuts wasted energy and manual work.

  • Lighting control with schedules, scenes and occupancy sensing
  • Air conditioning and ventilation control
  • Energy and power monitoring
  • Monitoring of pumps, generators, tanks and other plant
  • Motorised gates, blinds and shutters
  • Control from wall panels, a central dashboard or your phone

02

CCTV surveillance

Good surveillance starts with camera placement. We survey the site, identify entrances, blind spots and lighting conditions, then specify cameras and recording to match, so footage is usable when you need it.

  • High-definition IP camera systems
  • Network video recorders sized for your retention period
  • Secure remote viewing on phone, tablet and desktop
  • Night vision and low-light coverage
  • Motion alerts and video analytics
  • Upgrades and takeovers of existing camera systems

03

Access control

Access control replaces keys with credentials you can issue, change and withdraw in seconds. You decide who can enter each area and at what times, and the system keeps a record of every entry.

  • Smart door locks and grill locks
  • Card, fob, PIN, mobile and biometric readers
  • Electric locks, door controllers and exit devices
  • Gate and barrier automation
  • Video intercom and visitor entry
  • Time and attendance reporting
  • Integration with CCTV and alarm systems
