About

The technology layer that finishes a building

Smart Building Finishes supplies and installs building automation, CCTV and access control for commercial and residential properties, island-wide across Jamaica.

What we believe

A building is not finished when the paint dries. It is finished when it is secure, comfortable and easy to manage. That last layer, the controls, cameras and door systems, is what we do.

We work with owners, developers, architects and contractors, on new builds and on properties that are already occupied. Either way the aim is the same: a system that suits how the building is used and that the people in it can operate with confidence.

Our approach

What you can expect from us

Designed for your site

No off-the-shelf packages. Each proposal follows a survey and is built around your layout, risks and budget.

Tidy workmanship

Concealed, labelled cabling and equipment mounted so it complements the building instead of spoiling it.

Clear handover

Your team is shown how to use the system, and you receive the documentation and credentials for it.

Ongoing support

We remain available after installation for servicing, changes and expansion as the property grows.
